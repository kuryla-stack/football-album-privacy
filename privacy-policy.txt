Album — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026
Owner contact: replace-with-owner-email@example.com

Album is a football sticker and card collection app. This policy explains what
the app stores and what it does not collect.

What Album stores

Album stores your card collection, duplicate counts, coins, mission and
achievement progress, statistics, settings, and whether onboarding has been
completed. This information is stored in Album's private app storage on your
device so that your collection is still there when you reopen the app.

Album does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email
address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or contacts.

No advertising, analytics, or cloud service

Album contains no advertisements, advertising identifiers, analytics, or crash
reporting service. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, login, or
cloud-storage SDK. Album uses no third-party service SDKs. Album does not send
your collection or device information to a developer server, and it does not
provide cloud sync.

The app's audio, card catalogue, and collection logic run locally. Network
access is not needed for the app's collection features. If you use Android's
copy or share tools yourself, the Android operating system may make the copied
collection code available to the destination you choose; that action is under
your control and is separate from Album's private storage.

Your choices and deletion

You can reset your collection and progress from Settings inside Album. You can
also remove all app data through Android settings. Uninstalling Album deletes
the data held in the app's private storage, subject to any device-level backup
or restore features that you control through Android.

Children and changes

Album is published for an adult audience and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone. If the app's data practices change, this
policy will be updated with a new effective date. Questions about this policy
can be sent to replace-with-owner-email@example.com.
